Creative direction

Build a catalog that feels like its own universe.

The goal is bigger than individual tracks: create a recognizable sound, form lasting collaborations, and keep expanding what emotional, futuristic music can be.

Make music that gives people somewhere to go—even when they never leave the room.

Mission objectives

Where the project is headed.

These goals guide the sound, the collaborations, and the kind of work Tristan wants to take on next.

01 / IDENTITY

Refine the signature sound

Develop a clear sonic identity built around atmosphere, emotion, melody, and futuristic detail.

02 / COLLABORATION

Create with more artists

Work with vocalists, producers, musicians, and visual artists who bring new energy to the universe.

03 / RELEASES

Build a consistent catalog

Release finished music more consistently while protecting the care and originality behind each track.

Creative orbit

How the work moves.

A simple cycle for turning an idea into something complete, shareable, and true to the original feeling.

Explore

Collect references, textures, chords, field recordings, and moods without forcing the outcome.

Shape

Find the core idea and build an arrangement that gives it room to breathe.

Refine

Strengthen the performance, details, transitions, and sonic depth.

Release

Finish the mix, master the track, and send the signal into the world.
